The cloudy.so look Serif headlines and a brighter blue button

Three panels with the same content. The first is bevel.css 0.6.0 as it ships. The other two take cloudy.so’s two big moves: a serif for display headings and a brighter, rounder primary button. Body text, labels and controls stay in Helvetica Neue in all three. Switch to dark in the navbar to see both themes.

Today, bevel.css 0.6.0

Helvetica Neue at 600 for every heading and the #0B65C2 primary with 2.0’s gloss. Left-aligned hero.

Cloudy look, system serif

Display headings in Iowan Old Style on Apple systems, Palatino Linotype on Windows and P052 or URW Palladio on Linux, so it keeps bevel’s no-web-fonts rule. The primary is #2A6AD8 with a brighter top edge, rounder corners and a soft blue glow under it. Centered hero.
